How GPA is calculated (BD board rules)

Marks map to grades: 80+ A+ (5.00), 70–79 A (4.00), 60–69 A− (3.50), 50–59 B (3.00), 40–49 C (2.00), 33–39 D (1.00), below 33 F (0). GPA = (sum of compulsory grade points + any 4th-subject points above 2.00) ÷ number of compulsory subjects, capped at 5.00. Failing any compulsory subject fails the whole result; failing the 4th subject does not. Golden A+ means A+ in every subject including the 4th.
